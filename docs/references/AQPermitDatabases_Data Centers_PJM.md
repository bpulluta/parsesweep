# Air Quality Permit Databases for Data Centers in PJM Territory

Here is a comprehensive table of air quality permit search websites for all 14 jurisdictions in the PJM Interconnection territory. PJM covers all or parts of Delaware, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Maryland, Michigan, New Jersey, North Carolina, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Tennessee, Virginia, West Virginia, and the District of Columbia.

## State-by-State Air Quality Permit Databases

- **Air Permit Search**: https://den.test.dnrec.delaware.gov/Search/PermitSearch.aspx

- **Alternative URL**: https://dnrec.delaware.gov/air/permitting/
- **Data Center Resources**: General air permits

## Key Highlights

### Virginia has the most comprehensive data center resource
Virginia DEQ maintains a dedicated webpage specifically for "Issued Air Permits for Data Centers," making it the easiest starting point for data center backup generator information in the PJM territory.

### Maryland and New Jersey have emergency generator-specific permits
Both states offer general permits specifically designed for emergency generators, which can streamline your search for backup generator specifications.

### High-priority states for data center backup generators
Based on recent permit activity, focus your search on:

- **Virginia** (Northern Virginia/Loudoun County area has the highest concentration)
- **Maryland** (Frederick County - significant data center development)
- **Pennsylvania** (growing data center activity)
- **Ohio** (multiple data center facilities)
